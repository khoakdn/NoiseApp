Noise Level Dashboard

Monitor and Improve Study Environments in the Fontys TQ Building

About the Project

This dashboard helps students and staff monitor real-time noise levels in different study zones within the Fontys TQ building. It promotes better focus and enhances the overall learning experience.

Why Use It?

  • 📊 Real-time Noise Data Visualization
  • 🧘 Improve Focus in Quiet Zones
  • 📍 Locate Ideal Study Areas Instantly
  • 🔔 Get Alerts When Noise Exceeds Limits
